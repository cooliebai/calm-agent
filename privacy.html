Calm agent: privacy policy

Calm agent is a private assistant used only by the members of one household. This policy describes how it handles Google account data.

What it accesses

  • Gmail, read only. It reads messages in the household's own accounts to find dates, deadlines and items that need attention. It cannot send, change or delete email.
  • Google Calendar events. It reads calendar events and creates or edits events on the household's behalf.

Where data is kept

Information the assistant keeps is stored on a server in the family's home. To understand a message or request, the relevant text is sent to an AI service provider for processing. Data is not sold, not used for advertising and not shared with anyone else.

Google API Services

Calm agent's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be withdrawn at any time from the Google Account permissions page, and stored data is deleted on request by the household member it belongs to.
